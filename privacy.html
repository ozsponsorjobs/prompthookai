Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 24, 2026 • Last Updated: September 24, 2026

At PromptHook AI (accessible from https://www.prompthookai.com/), safeguarding the privacy and digital autonomy of our visitors is our paramount commitment. This comprehensive Privacy Policy document outlines the categories of data collected, logged, and processed by PromptHook AI, as well as the rights granted to our global users under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Information We Do and Do Not Collect

PromptHook AI is engineered from the ground up as a zero-friction, static developer resource. We do not require account registration to view, copy, or inject parameters into our prompt blueprints.

  • Client-Side Parameter Injection: All parameter substitutions performed in our prompt sandboxes occur 100% locally within your browser's JavaScript V8 runtime. We do not transmit, log, or store the proprietary source code, internal system prompts, or configuration parameters you enter into our sandboxes.
  • Contact Inquiries: If you contact us directly via our verified Contact Portal or email, we may collect your name, email address, and the specific contents of your correspondence to resolve your inquiry.
  • Standard Server Log Files: Like standard web hosts (e.g., Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages), we may record anonymized log data including Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and total clicks to analyze trends and defend against DDoS attacks.

2. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is an authorized third-party vendor on our site. Google uses cookies, specifically known as DART cookies, to serve advertisements to our website visitors based upon their visits to prompthookai.com and other websites across the Internet.

You may elect to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Our advertising partners may utilize automated cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons within their respective ads that appear on PromptHook AI. These technologies automatically receive your IP address when ad delivery occurs to measure campaign effectiveness and personalize promotional material according to your technical interests.

3. Cookie Preferences & Local Storage

We use localized browser storage (HTML5 localStorage) strictly to record your consent preferences regarding non-essential cookies. You can revoke, reconfigure, or delete this consent at any moment by clearing your browser's local cache or clicking our Cookie Preferences toggle.

4. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Data Subject Rights

For visitors situated within the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess enforceable legal rights under Chapter III of the GDPR:

  • The Right of Access: You are entitled to obtain confirmation as to whether personal data concerning you is processed.
  • The Right to Rectification: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • The Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the right to request the deletion of your personal records.
  • The Right to Restrict Processing & Object: You can object to our processing of your personal information under certain circumstances.
  • The Right to Data Portability: You may request transmission of any data we hold directly to another organization.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Privacy Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers possess the right to request disclosure of personal data categories collected, request immediate deletion, and exercise the Right to Opt-Out of the Sale of Personal Information. PromptHook AI affirms that we do not sell, rent, or trade user personal data to any third-party data broker.

6. Third-Party Links & AI APIs

Our prompt blueprints contain instructional references to third-party artificial intelligence engines (e.g., Anthropic Claude, OpenAI ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Google Gemini). PromptHook AI does not control and assumes no liability for the external data privacy practices, data training policies, or terms of service enforced by these independent AI providers.
